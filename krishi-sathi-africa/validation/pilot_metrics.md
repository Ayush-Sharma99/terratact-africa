# Pilot Key Performance Indicators & Measurement Scorecard

**Target:** Krishi Sathi Season 1 Pilot (Mbeya / Songwe, Tanzania)  
**Sample:** 350 Treatment Smallholders vs. 150 Matched Control Smallholders  

---

## 1. Leading Indicators (In-Season Execution)

| KPI ID | Metric Name | Measurement Tool | Target Benchmark | Minimum Threshold |
|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| **KPI-01** | Lead Farmer Profiling Rate | Mobile App Sync Log | 100% of 350 treatment plots mapped | 85% mapped |
| **KPI-02** | Plan Comprehension Score | Post-Visit Follow-Up Survey | $\ge 85\%$ of farmers explain rationale | 70% |
| **KPI-03** | Acreage Allocation Compliance | Field Boundary Verification | $\ge 70\%$ adopt recommended legume split | 50% |
| **KPI-04** | Input Depot Stock Availability | Weekly Depot Inventory Audit | 100% certified seed in stock at planting | 90% |
| **KPI-05** | SMS Weather Alert Delivery | Gateway Delivery Receipts | $\ge 95\%$ successful SMS delivery | 85% |

---

## 2. Lagging Indicators (Harvest & Commercial Impact)

| KPI ID | Metric Name | Measurement Tool | Target Benchmark | Minimum Threshold |
|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| **KPI-06** | Net Farm Income Increase | Endline Economic Survey | $\ge +35\%$ vs Control Cohort | $+20\%$ vs Control |
| **KPI-07** | Legume Yield Differential | Crop Cut Yield Measurement | $\ge 550$ kg/acre Common Bean | $400$ kg/acre |
| **KPI-08** | Loan Repayment Rate | AMCOS Credit Records | $\ge 92\%$ seasonal input loan recovery | $80\%$ |
| **KPI-09** | Co-op Aggregation Volume | AMCOS Warehouse Weighbridge | $\ge +25\%$ increase in legume tonnage | $+15\%$ |
| **KPI-10** | Enterprise Contract Renewal | Signed Co-op Agreement | $\ge 2$ of 3 AMCOS renew for Season 2 | 1 AMCOS renews |
