# 30 / 60 / 90-Day Execution Roadmap

**Execution Phase:** Phase 1 Market Entry & Pilot Preparation  
**Anchor Hub:** Mbeya, Tanzania  
**Target Milestone:** 500-Farmer Field Deployment  

---

## 1. 30-Day Sprint (Reconnaissance & Institutional MoUs)
- [ ] **TARI Uyole Engagement:** Conduct formal partnership meeting with TARI Uyole Centre Director in Mbeya; negotiate research collaboration MoU and access to Southern Highland trial data archives.
- [ ] **Cooperative Discovery:** Conduct 25 in-depth qualitative interviews with AMCOS chairpersons across Mbeya Rural, Mbozi, and Rungwe districts.
- [ ] **Seed Partner Alignment:** Audit certified legume seed inventory with Tanseed International and Beula Seed Company; confirm availability of Uyole 03 bean and Uyole Soya seed batches.
- [ ] **Legal Formation:** Submit business registration filings to BRELA for local Tanzanian subsidiary; initiate PDPA data controller registration with PDPC.

---

## 2. 60-Day Sprint (Data Pipelines & Software MVP)
- [ ] **Spatial Data Pipeline:** Implement automated cloud API connector querying iSDAsoil (30m) and CHIRPS rainfall for Southern Highland coordinates.
- [ ] **Hybrid Engine Implementation:** Code the Stage 1 Agronomic Exclusion Rules and Stage 3 SciPy Linear Programming acreage optimizer.
- [ ] **Swahili Field Agent App:** Build offline-first Flutter/Android tablet application with Swahili UI for AMCOS extension officers.
- [ ] **SMS Integration:** Configure Africa’s Talking SMS gateway for automated planting window and agronomic alert broadcasts.
- [ ] **Cooperative MoUs:** Sign formal pilot agreements with 3 selected AMCOS boards (target: 500 member farmers).

---

## 3. 90-Day Sprint (Field Deployment & Pilot Kickoff)
- [ ] **Co-op Lead Farmer Training:** Run intensive 3-day train-the-trainer workshops for 10 AMCOS Lead Farmers on tablet operation and farmer consultation.
- [ ] **Pre-Season Farmer Profiling:** Enumerate 500 pilot farmers; map plot boundaries; generate customized Swahili acreage diversification plans.
- [ ] **Input Pre-Clearance:** Verify physical delivery of certified legume seeds and agricultural lime to AMCOS village depots.
- [ ] **Planting Launch:** Dispatch seasonal onset SMS alerts upon confirmed initiation of *Masika* rains; supervise field planting compliance.
