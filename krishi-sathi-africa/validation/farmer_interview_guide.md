# Smallholder Farmer Qualitative Discovery Questionnaire

**Target Demographic:** Smallholder farmers (1.5–3.5 acres) in Mbeya/Songwe, Tanzania  
**Methodology:** Non-leading, ethnographic discovery interview (Swahili)  
**Consent Protocol:** Verbal Swahili informed consent (*ridhaa ya kushiriki*) before questioning.

---

## 1. Farming System & Acreage History
1. *Ni mazao gani uliyopanda msimu uliopita, na ulitenga ekari ngapi kwa kila zao?* (What crops did you grow last season, and how many acres did you allocate to each?)
2. *Je, uamuzi wa kuchagua zao gani upande hutegemea nini hasa?* (What specifically drives your decision on which crop to plant?)
3. *Je, unalima mahindi yote kwa ajili ya chakula cha nyumbani au unauza kiasi gani sokoni?* (Do you grow all your maize for home food or what proportion do you sell?)

## 2. Soil Health & Input Realities
4. *Je, umewahi kupima udongo wa shamba lako maabarani? Kama hapana, ni kwanini?* (Have you ever had your soil tested in a laboratory? If no, why?)
5. *Je, unaona mabadiliko gani katika rutuba ya udongo wako kwa miaka mitano iliyopita?* (What changes have you noticed in your soil fertility over the last five years?)
6. *Je, unatumia mbolea gani, unainunua wapi, na gharama zake zimeathiri vipi faida yako?* (What fertilizers do you use, where do you buy them, and how have costs affected your profit?)

## 3. Alternative Crops & Risk Perceptions
7. *Je, umewahi kufikiria kupunguza ekari za mahindi na kupanda maharage au soya zaidi? Kama ndiyo au hapana, kwanini?* (Have you ever considered reducing maize acres to grow more beans or soybeans? Why or why not?)
8. *Ni nini kinachokupa hofu kubwa zaidi unapotaka kujaribu zao jipya au mbegu mpya?* (What gives you the greatest fear when trying a new crop or new seed?)
9. *Ukipata mavuno makubwa ya maharage au soya, unauza wapi na unalipwaje?* (If you get a large bean or soybean harvest, where do you sell and how are you paid?)

## 4. Trust & Information Channels
10. *Nani unayemwamini zaidi anapokupa ushauri wa kilimo (Afisa Ugani, Muuza maduka ya pembejeo, viongozi wa AMCOS, au wakulima wenzako)?* (Whom do you trust most for farming advice: extension officer, agro-dealer, AMCOS leaders, or fellow farmers?)
11. *Je, unatumia simu ya aina gani (kitochi au simu ya kioo/smartphone)? Je, mtandao unapatikana vizuri shambani kwako?* (What phone do you use: feature phone or smartphone? Is network reliable at your farm?)
