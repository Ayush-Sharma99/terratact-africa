## 1. Executive Directive

This workspace contains the independent due-diligence, market discovery, competitive intelligence, and venture strategy package for **Krishi Sathi**—evaluating whether and how an early-stage crop recommendation concept can be transformed into a viable, high-impact, commercially sustainable decision-support venture in Sub-Saharan Africa.

The assessment operates under **strict investment committee skepticism**:
- Hypotheses are rejected unless supported by primary or peer-reviewed evidence.
- Every material factual claim is logged in the source register with exact URLs, publication dates, and evidence classifications.
- Technology is evaluated against real-world smallholder economics, field infrastructure, and verified adoption barriers.

## 3. Multidisciplinary Research Team Roles

The due-diligence package reflects findings synthesized across 10 specialized functional lenses:

1. **VC Analyst (Emerging Markets / Africa):** Unit economics, venture backability, fundability, and exit dynamics.
2. **African Agricultural Economist:** Smallholder farm systems, household labor, food security, and gross margin trade-offs.
3. **Regional Market-Entry Consultant:** Ease of operating, regulatory hurdles, infrastructure in East vs. West vs. North vs. Southern Africa.
4. **Agronomist (Soil & Crop Systems):** Soil chemistry, pH constraints, rainfall patterns, water balance, and nutrient deficiencies.
5. **AI/ML Research Scientist:** Spatial cross-validation, synthetic dataset critique, hybrid agronomic modeling, and OOD handling.
6. **Development Economist:** Extension failure modes, input subsidy interactions, gender barriers, and poverty metrics.
7. **B2B/B2G GTM Strategist:** Agro-dealer networks, off-taker supply chains, government agricultural programs, and donor funding.
8. **Product Strategist (Low-Connectivity/Multilingual):** Feature phones, USSD/IVR, WhatsApp bots, voice UI, and offline caching.
9. **Technical Due-Diligence Analyst:** Open spatial datasets (iSDAsoil, CHIRPS, NASA POWER), API costs, and IP licensing.
10. **Investigative Research Analyst:** Verification of primary sources, citation integrity, and falsification of marketing claims.
