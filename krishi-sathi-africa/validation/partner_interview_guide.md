# Institutional Partner Discovery Questionnaire

**Target Stakeholders:** AMCOS Board Chairpersons, Commercial Off-Takers, Agro-Dealers, TARI Researchers  
**Objective:** Validate B2B willingness-to-pay, distribution bottlenecks, and contractual terms.

---

## 1. AMCOS Cooperative Board Leadership
1. What percentage of your members successfully repay their seasonal input loans, and what causes the defaults?
2. How does the AMCOS earn revenue from member crop marketing (what is your statutory cess or commission per bag)?
3. If an agronomic software tool increased your members' bean/soybean production by 30%, how would that impact the cooperative's financial balance sheet?
4. Would the AMCOS board be willing to allocate TZS 4,000–5,000 (\$1.50–\$2.00) per active member per season from marketing revenues to equip your extension officers with plot-level decision software?

## 2. Commercial Off-Takers (Feed Millers & Grain Aggregators)
5. What are your annual procurement targets for soybeans and common beans, and what percentage do you currently secure from local smallholders?
6. What are your biggest challenges with smallholder supply chains (volume consistency, moisture content, variety mixing, broken contracts)?
7. Would you be willing to sign forward-purchase contracts with AMCOS cooperatives if an agronomic platform verified plot acreage, planting dates, and projected harvest volumes?

## 3. Local Input Distributors & Seed Companies
8. What certified legume seed varieties (Uyole 03, Uyole Soya) do you stock, and what lead time is required to supply 500 farmers in Mbeya/Songwe?
9. How do you handle counterfeit seed infiltration in rural retail shops, and how could digital batch verification protect your brand?
