## 1. Executive Context & Mentor Discussion Origins

Following the completion of the Stage 1 due diligence package (which established that a direct-to-consumer ML crop classifier is unviable in Sub-Saharan Africa and provisionally proposed a B2B2C legume diversification model in the Southern Highlands of Tanzania), informal feedback from a mentor discussion introduced three new strategic angles:

1. **Multi-Season & Sustainable Farm Planning:** Moving beyond a one-time planting recommendation toward a dynamic, season-by-season decision tool that guides farmers on selecting different seeds or varieties across successive cropping seasons throughout the year.
2. **High-Value Specialty Crops & Mushrooms:** Exploring whether TerraTact should incorporate or pivot toward high-value niche crops, specifically mentioning mushrooms and an ambiguous reference to "Gucci / Gucchi" (morel) mushrooms.
3. **Macroeconomic, Currency & Operating Cost Re-Evaluation:** Re-opening the African country screening to compare Kenya, Tanzania, Ethiopia, and Nigeria with explicit focus on local operational affordability, purchasing power, foreign exchange (FX) risk, and currency devaluation dynamics.

## 3. Explicit Ambiguity Register

### Ambiguity A: The Definition of "Different Seeds Throughout the Year"
- *Plausible Meaning 1 (Crop Rotation):* Rotating crops between major seasons (e.g., *Masika* long rains maize $\rightarrow$ *Vuli* short rains beans/cowpeas) to break pest cycles and restore soil nutrients.
- *Plausible Meaning 2 (Maturity & Varietal Switching):* Recommending short-duration vs. medium-duration hybrid seed varieties based on delayed seasonal rainfall onset forecasts.
- *Plausible Meaning 3 (Continuous Succession Horticulture):* Year-round staggered vegetable seed planting for farmers with shallow-well or furrow irrigation access.
- *Research Action:* Investigate whether FAO crop calendars, national research institutes, and seed distributors already provide varietal calendars, and identify where digital decision support adds demonstrable value.

### Ambiguity B: The "Gucci Mushroom" Enigma
- *Plausible Meaning 1 (Gucchi / Morel Mushrooms - Morchella):* A luxury wild mushroom harvested in the Western Himalayas (Kashmir, Himachal) and temperate forests, commanding \$150–\$400+/kg dried. Highly prized in Indian gastronomy.
- *Plausible Meaning 2 (Gourmet Cultivated Mushrooms):* Commercially cultivated saprophytes such as Oyster (*Pleurotus*), Shiitake (*Lentinula*), or Button (*Agaricus*) grown on agricultural crop waste.
- *Plausible Meaning 3 (Indigenous African Edible Mushrooms):* Wild mycorrhizal species (e.g., *Termitomyces titanicus*, chanterelles) collected seasonally in African Miombo woodlands.
- *Research Action:* Conduct an exhaustive botanical and economic feasibility study in Phase 4. We will evaluate *Morchella* biology, climate triggers, and Chinese artificial cultivation techniques, alongside tropical *Pleurotus* economics.

### Ambiguity C: The "Currency & Low-Cost" Presumption
- *The Trap:* Conflating a weak nominal exchange rate (e.g., 1,500 NGN/\$ or 115 ETB/\$) with a "cheap" operational environment.
- *Macro Reality:* In emerging markets, aggressive currency devaluations are almost universally coupled with soaring domestic inflation, collapsing smallholder purchasing power, surging transport/fuel tariffs, and severe foreign exchange illiquidity.
- *Research Action:* Perform an objective purchasing-power-parity (PPP), inflation-adjusted, and FX-repatriation audit across Kenya, Tanzania, Ethiopia, and Nigeria in Phase 5 and Phase 8.
