# Go-To-Market & Channel Distribution Strategy

**Target Geography:** Tanzania (Southern Highlands - Mbeya, Songwe, Iringa)  
**Distribution Archetype:** Institutional B2B2C / Phygital Intermediation  
**Primary Anchor:** Agricultural Marketing Co-operative Societies (AMCOS)  

---

## 1. The Channel Problem in Rural Africa

Direct-to-consumer (B2C) customer acquisition in African agriculture fails due to:
- Dispersed rural geography and high physical travel costs.
- Low rural digital literacy and mistrust of unfamiliar digital products.
- The "First-Mile Payment Friction" (farmers will not enter mobile money PINs for software subscriptions).

### The Solution: Institutional Intermediation via AMCOS
Rather than acquiring individual farmers, Krishi Sathi signs **enterprise B2B contracts with AMCOS cooperative unions**. 

```text
               THE B2B2C GO-TO-MARKET FUNNEL:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Enterprise Institutional Level: Krishi Sathi contracts with AMCOS Board.  │
│    • Value: Yield forecasting, loan recovery & produce aggregation volume.  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Field Extension Level: Co-op assigns 2-5 Lead Farmers / Ward Agronomists.│
│    • Tool: Krishi Sathi Android Tablet App (Offline-capable SQLite sync).   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Member Smallholder Level: Lead Farmer visits members during pre-planting.│
│    • Interaction: Captures plot GPS, generates Swahili rotation plan,       │
│      sends SMS summary to farmer phone, and records input orders.           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Institutional Partnership Strategy

To build sustainable distribution without heavy capital expenditure, Krishi Sathi aligns with four key ecosystem partners:

1. **Agricultural Research Partner (TARI Uyole Centre):** Validates agronomic recommendation algorithms, grants access to regional trial datasets, and provides official scientific endorsement.
2. **Cooperative Authority (TCDC & SAGCOT Centre):** Endorses Krishi Sathi as an approved digital productivity tool for Southern Highland cooperatives.
3. **Certified Seed Distributors (Tanseed International, Beula Seed):** Pre-aligns seed inventories with AMCOS depots to ensure recommended certified legume varieties are physically in stock prior to the rains.
4. **Commercial Off-Takers (Domestic Animal Feed & Grain Millers):** Secures forward purchase contracts for aggregated soybean and bean harvests.
