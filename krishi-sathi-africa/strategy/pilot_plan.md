# Phase X: Phased Pilot Design & Field Validation Protocol

**Pilot Territory:** Mbeya Rural and Mbozi Districts (Southern Highlands, Tanzania)  
**Pilot Target:** 500 Smallholder Households across 3 AMCOS Cooperatives  
**Duration:** 1 Full Cropping Season (*Masika*, November 2026 – June 2027)  

---

## 1. Pilot Architecture & Cohort Design

To isolate the causal economic impact of Krishi Sathi’s decision engine, the pilot implements a **quasi-experimental matched-cohort design**:

```text
               PILOT COHORT COMPARISON FRAMEWORK:
┌──────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┐
│  TREATMENT GROUP (N = 350 Farmers)   │   CONTROL GROUP (N = 150 Farmers)    │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Receives Krishi Sathi Swahili      │ • Continues standard farming practice│
│   Acreage Allocation & Soil Plan     │   (unassisted continuous maize).     │
│ • AMCOS pre-cleared certified seed   │ • Procures inputs through standard   │
│   (Uyole 03 / Uyole Soya) + Lime.    │   village channels as usual.         │
│ • Seasonal weather alerts via SMS.   │ • Surveyed at baseline & endline for │
│ • Supervised by Co-op Lead Farmers.  │   yield and gross margin comparison. │
└──────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. Pilot Implementation Timeline

- **Month 1 (Pre-Season Alignment - Nov):**
  - Sign formal pilot MoU with 3 AMCOS leadership boards in Mbeya/Songwe.
  - Baseline survey enumeration (land size, historical maize yield, baseline income).
  - Pre-stock certified seed (Uyole 03) and lime in co-op depots.
- **Month 2 (Planting Window - Dec):**
  - Lead Farmers map plot boundaries via tablet app; generate Swahili diversification plans.
  - Farmers plant recommended split (e.g., 60% Maize / 40% Common Bean).
- **Months 3–5 (In-Season Monitoring - Jan–Apr):**
  - Dekadal satellite rainfall tracking via CHIRPS; send timely weeding and top-dressing SMS alerts.
  - Extension agent field visits to track germination and pest status (FAW).
- **Month 6 (Harvest & Aggregation - May–Jun):**
  - Measure harvested crop yields and grain quality.
  - AMCOS aggregates legume harvest and settles forward purchase contracts with off-takers.
  - Endline survey execution to compute net household profit differential.

---

## 3. Go / No-Go Decision Scorecard

At the conclusion of Month 6, the investment committee reviews results against four strict criteria:

| Indicator | Target Threshold | Minimum Acceptable | Red Flag / Halt |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Recommendation Adoption Rate** | $\ge 70\%$ of farmers | $50\%$ of farmers | $< 40\%$ |
| **Net Income Differential** | $\ge +35\%$ vs Control | $\ge +20\%$ vs Control | $< +10\%$ |
| **AMCOS Contract Renewal** | $\ge 2$ Co-ops renew | $1$ Co-op renews | $0$ Co-ops renew |
| **Prediction Accuracy (Yield Rank)**| $\ge 80\%$ concordance | $\ge 65\%$ concordance | $< 50\%$ |
